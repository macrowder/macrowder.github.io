Read Aloud Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

In short: Read Aloud has no accounts or ads, and the developer never receives your data. Your text, files and photos stay on your phone. Two things go to Google: anonymous diagnostics from Google's on-device text recognition library, and, only if you turn on optional online reading with your own Gemini key, page photos sent to Google to be read.

What the app keeps on your phone

  • The text you read (from photos, files or pasted text), its title, and your reading spot.
  • Your settings, such as voice, speed, text size and theme.
  • If you enter one, your Google Gemini API key, stored encrypted with the Android Keystore.
  • Photos you take in the app, kept only while they are being read. A page photo that couldn't be read online is kept until it can be, then deleted.
  • A short troubleshooting file about the last photos read. It stays on the phone unless you choose to share it yourself.

Your settings, your key and any saved photos are left out of Android cloud backups and phone-to-phone transfers.

Camera

The camera is used only to photograph pages you want read aloud. Text is recognized on the phone with Google ML Kit's on-device text recognition. Your photos and their text are not sent anywhere for this.

Diagnostics from Google ML Kit

The ML Kit library sends Google anonymous diagnostics: device and Android version details, the app's name and version, performance and error information, and a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you or your device. Google uses this to measure and improve ML Kit. It does not include your photos or text. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

Files and shared text

PDF, Word and text files you open, and text shared to the app, are read on the phone. They are not uploaded anywhere.

Optional online reading (Google Gemini)

Online reading is off unless you enter your own Gemini API key in Settings. When it is on, each page photo you take or pick is sent directly from your phone to Google's Gemini API, using your key, so the text can be read more accurately. The developer does not receive these photos.

Google's handling of that data is covered by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products. Only use online reading for book pages, not personal documents. Google's terms require Gemini API users to be 18 or older.

Voices

Speech comes from the text-to-speech engine chosen in your phone's settings. If you choose an online voice, that engine may send the text being read to its provider (for example Google) to make the speech. That is covered by the provider's own privacy policy.

Sharing and selling

Read Aloud does not sell your data, show ads, or include advertising or tracking tools. The developer receives no data from the app.

Deleting your data

Delete any saved item from the app's home screen. Remove your Gemini key in Settings. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on the phone. To manage or delete a Gemini key itself, use Google AI Studio.

Children

Read Aloud is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
